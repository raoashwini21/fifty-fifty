# Fifty-Fifty

**Minesweeper, flipped.** You hide the mines. A sweeper who thinks out loud tries to survive.

> Built for Hackyard Yard #7 · theme: **Remix** · work in progress

## The idea

In the game everyone remembers, you tap squares and hope.
Here the roles swap: you place the mines, press Go, and the sweeper does the tapping.

It's smart. It reads the numbers and never steps on a mine it could have worked out.
You win only by hiding mines so cleverly that it's forced to guess, and guesses wrong.
